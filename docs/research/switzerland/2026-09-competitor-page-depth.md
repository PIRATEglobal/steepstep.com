# Schweizer Markt, Seiten- und Content-Tiefe

Geprüft: 12. September 2026. Read-only SERP and public-page review to decide which Steepstep routes earn indexation.

## Live examples

- [Physio Online, Proalp Baden](https://www.physioproalp.ch/physio-online), crawled 2 months ago. Makes online service explicit, names sports physiotherapy and shows a starting price of CHF 50 / EUR 55 for 30 minutes. This answers format, audience, and price early.
- [Vivid Physio Zürich, online booking](https://www.vividphysio.ch/terminbuchung), crawled 3 days ago. Connects direct booking to sports-ground work, injury evaluation, athlete support, prevention, and recovery. The useful pattern is a concrete service context, not a generic sport directory.
- [Physio Sports Zürich](https://physiosports.ch/), crawled 2 weeks ago. Leads with evidence-based physiotherapy and sport physiotherapy, names the founders, and explains VALD diagnostics and therapist choice. Named people, method, and measurable assessment create trust.
- [Sportphysio Base Engiadina, prices](https://www.sportphysiobase.ch/preise), crawled 2 months ago. Publishes a separate price context for physiotherapeutic advice or instruction by phone/online. A Steepstep pricing page should explain exactly what remote advice includes and where clinical treatment differs.
- [Hirslanden Medical Center Wankdorf, physiotherapy](https://www.hirslanden.ch/de/medical-center-wankdorf/physiotherapie.html), crawled yesterday. Combines sports physiotherapy with prevention, acute sideline care, and return-to-play strategy, plus CHF pricing and a path for cases missing from online booking. This is a strong model for safety boundaries and escalation copy.

## Decision for Steepstep routes

Keep the Sport hub, because it gives the brand a meaningful doorway into the stated sports strength and can explain how online suitability is decided. Keep activity pages only if each answers a distinct activity question with first-hand method, load variables, safety boundary, and a next action. The current Laufen, Skifahren, Fussball, and Wandern pages remain too similar: each repeats the same suitability paragraph, first-appointment paragraph, price, and CTA. Activity-specific opening copy alone does not earn four indexed pages.

Recommended release shape: retain /sport and one or two pages with verified demand and real clinician examples; merge the rest into the hub until each has unique evidence, a clear answer passage, and at least one useful preparation or progression section. Do not create more sport slugs merely to cover a keyword list.

## What Steepstep can own

The defensible gap is a Swiss-first, private-pay remote assessment for active adults that makes the decision boundary visible: what can be observed by video, what the written next step contains, when to stop and seek local care, and what the package includes. This is more distinctive than repeating “sport physiotherapy” or copying clinic service menus.
