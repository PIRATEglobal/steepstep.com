# Live brand research: Powerflow with Natty and Steepstep

**Research date:** 12 September 2026
**Scope:** Live website review for the Swiss physiotherapy website brief
**Method:** Read-only review of public pages and visible image references

## Executive read

Powerflow with Natty provides valuable first-party material for the founder story, sports credibility, and visual direction. The site identifies Natalja “Natty” Krol as a physiotherapist with a focus on sports physiotherapy and says she worked for years in competitive sport with basketball and football players. It also gives a public contact email, Swiss phone number, and Suhr location. These details are useful source material for an About page, subject to the owner confirming that they remain current and approving the exact wording.

The current Powerflow site is positioned around emotional wellbeing, bodywork, and THE HEALING KEY®, with a direct-to-founder voice, long-form personal story, programme cards, a free Clarity Call, and testimonial-led proof. The new site should carry over the human presence, clear founder voice, real photography, and continuity of care. It should leave behind unsupported healing, nervous-system, speed, and transformation claims. For a regulated physiotherapy offer, the visible proof must start with professional identity, scope, Swiss authorisation, method, suitability, and the concrete appointment.

No dependable public result for a Swiss physiotherapy website named “Steepstep” was found in the search pass, and no matching local files exist in this project. The parent task should supply the exact URL before any comparison or reuse decision is made.

## Sourced Powerflow findings

### Founder and professional material

The public [Powerflow home page](https://www.powerflow-with-natty.com/) says the service is “Powerflow with Natty,” uses first-person copy, and presents Natty as the guide. The [About page](https://www.powerflow-with-natty.com/%C3%BCber-mich) states:

- `Ich bin Physiotherapeutin mit Schwerpunkt Sportphysiotherapie`.
- She worked for years in competitive sport with basketball players, football players, and people who give their all.
- Her story includes personal exhaustion, a turn toward slower attention and body awareness, and later training as a mental coach and Inside Yoga teacher.
- Her current work combines physiotherapy knowledge, competitive-sport experience, THE HEALING KEY®, body awareness, and movement.
- The footer identifies `Suhr, Switzerland`, `info@powerflow-with-natty.com`, and `+41 78 306 74 21`.

These are website statements, not independently verified credentials. Before reuse, confirm the exact education, protected title, canton authorisation or registration, current practice entity, and whether sports-team work can be named publicly. Do not infer a licence or specialist title from the page alone.

### Current argument and conversion pattern

The Powerflow home page uses this sequence:

1. A direct emotional problem: `Erschöpft, überfordert, ausgebrannt?`
2. A plain invitation: return to inner calm and physical strength.
3. A long personal explanation of the problem and method.
4. Three programme cards, each with an action labelled `Loslegen`.
5. A free 30-minute `Clarity Call`.
6. A long testimonial using a named first name, detailed session description, and strong result language.
7. Direct contact details.

For the Swiss physio site, keep the first-person warmth and direct contact. Replace the long method-first explanation with a shorter care argument: the person’s activity problem, the named physiotherapist, the first appointment, what can be assessed online, what happens next, and when in-person care is needed. Use a paid first appointment as the main action, with a fit route only if it is operationally staffed.

### Copy and trust lessons

Useful patterns to retain:

- Personal language that makes the practitioner visible early.
- A clear reason for the work, rather than a faceless clinic description.
- A simple programme structure with a single action.
- Public email, phone, and Swiss location.
- Real photography and a visible person behind the service.

Patterns to avoid for regulated physiotherapy:

- Claims that THE HEALING KEY® can dissolve deep patterns “within kürzester Zeit.”
- Claims that the method automatically changes thinking, feeling, or reactions.
- “Das Ergebnis” language that promises people will feel lighter, clearer, and freer.
- Anonymous or lightly identified testimonials that claim a lasting result.
- Presenting coaching, yoga, bodywork, and physiotherapy as one undifferentiated clinical service.
- A free call as the only meaningful conversion when the product is a paid clinical appointment.

## Proposed Swiss brand story

The strongest bridge from the existing brand to a Swiss physio-first service is:

> You know your body and your sport. When pain or an injury interrupts it, you need a qualified person who can make the next step clear and stay with you while you take it.

This keeps the founder’s lived relationship with pressure and performance, while putting physiotherapy and the customer’s activity first. The page should not lead with healing language. The brand story should answer three questions in order:

1. Why this practitioner understands active people and the pressure to return.
2. What her professional physiotherapy training and sports experience mean for the care.
3. How the online service helps a suitable person decide, practise, and progress safely.

### About page structure

Recommended sections:

1. A real portrait and one-sentence role: `Physiotherapeutin mit Schwerpunkt Sportphysiotherapie`.
2. `Womit ich Sie begleite`: sport and movement cases that fit the service.
3. `Was ich mitbringe`: verified training, sports experience, language, Swiss authorisation, and current service region.
4. `Wie ich arbeite`: listen, assess what video can show, agree a functional goal, guide progression, and refer when in-person care is needed.
5. `Warum ich diese Arbeit mache`: a short, owner-approved personal paragraph derived from the existing story.
6. Contact and booking action.

### Proposed copy directions, pending native edit and credential approval

**Hero:** `Physiotherapie für Menschen, die wieder in Bewegung kommen möchten.`

**Founder introduction:** `Ich weiss, wie viel Bewegung mit dem eigenen Leben zu tun hat. Als Physiotherapeutin mit Schwerpunkt Sportphysiotherapie begleite ich Sie dabei, wieder Vertrauen in Ihren Körper und in Ihren nächsten Schritt zu gewinnen.`

**Method:** `Wir beginnen mit Ihrer Situation, Ihrem Ziel und dem, was Sie wieder tun möchten. Im Online-Termin prüfen wir, was sich sicher beurteilen und begleiten lässt. Wenn Sie eine Untersuchung vor Ort brauchen, sagen wir Ihnen das klar.`

**Trust:** `Sie wissen, wer Sie begleitet, welche Ausbildung und Bewilligung diese Person mitbringt und was der Termin kostet, bevor Sie buchen.`

Avoid using the current Powerflow sentence `Veränderung passiert nicht, wenn du kämpfst – sondern, wenn du loslässt` on the physio site. It belongs to the existing emotional-coaching positioning, uses an unhelpful binary, and conflicts with a measured clinical progression story.

## Proposed sports-specific pages

The pages should be built from real search and customer evidence, not from a directory of sports. Start with the activities where the research memo already shows a credible demand signal and the team has stated strength.

### 1. Running

**Job:** Understand whether to keep running, reduce load, or pause, then build back with a plan.
**H1 direction:** `Wieder laufen, mit einem Plan, der zu Ihnen passt.`
**Sections:** common trigger, online suitability, what the first appointment covers, return-to-run progression, warning signs, package, FAQ.
**Visual:** the runner preparing at home or outdoors, with room for an editorial caption. Avoid a dramatic knee-holding pose.

### 2. Skiing

**Job:** Return to skiing after a knee, ankle, or shoulder problem and understand the next safe progression.
**H1 direction:** `Zurück auf die Piste, Schritt für Schritt.`
**Sections:** season timing, return criteria, strength and load progression, what cannot be assessed remotely, in-person referral route.
**Visual:** equipment preparation or controlled movement, not an injury scene or extreme-action image.

### 3. Football

**Job:** Return to training and play with a clear progression and communication with coach or club where appropriate.
**H1 direction:** `Zurück ins Training. Mit Klarheit für den nächsten Schritt.`
**Sections:** training load, field movement observation, return-to-play decision boundaries, referral needs, package.
**Visual:** calm preparation or change-of-direction drill in a real setting.

### 4. Hiking and mountain movement

**Job:** Prepare for a planned hike or manage pain and load without losing the season.
**H1 direction:** `Damit der nächste Aufstieg wieder möglich wird.`
**Sections:** terrain and load, footwear or equipment context, pacing, progression, red flags, when online care is unsuitable.
**Visual:** packing, footwear, or a controlled home exercise with alpine context used sparingly.

### 5. Cycling and climbing, demand tests

Create only after the first cohort shows enough traffic or qualified leads. Use specific problems such as saddle-related pain, return after a fall, grip or shoulder load, and training-volume changes. Do not publish thin “sport pages” with only swapped nouns.

## Opportunity pages beyond sport

The strongest adjacent page remains recurring neck, back, and shoulder pain for desk-based professionals. Its promise should be work tolerance and a plan for the next flare-up, with a separate offer and evidence. Do not mix it into the sports page navigation until the first sports cohort proves safe delivery and acceptable acquisition economics.

Other later opportunities:

- post-discharge continuity with a named referral partner;
- English-language care for international professionals, once patient-location and operating rules are confirmed;
- women returning to activity after pregnancy, only with documented specialist competence;
- fall prevention and balance, only with the right clinical pathway and target population.

## Recommended information architecture addition

Add a small `Sport & Bewegung` hub only when at least two pages have real content. Keep the navigation shallow:

- `/sport/laufen`
- `/sport/skifahren`
- `/sport/fussball`
- `/sport/wandern`
- `/arbeit/nacken-ruecken-schulter` later

Each page should share the same safety, first-appointment, price, and booking components, while the opening problem, examples, functional measure, and imagery are specific to the activity.
