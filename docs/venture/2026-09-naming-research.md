# Naming research for the Swiss online physiotherapy practice

**Research date:** 12 September 2026
**Market:** Switzerland, German-speaking Switzerland first
**Language:** Swiss Standard German (`de-CH`)
**Scope:** working brand names for a clinician-led online physiotherapy practice for active adults. This is a naming recommendation, not a trademark, company-name, or domain clearance.

## Decision in one paragraph

The strongest direction is **Klarlauf** with a plain descriptor such as **Klarlauf · Online-Physiotherapie für Bewegung in der Schweiz**. It is short, easy to say in German, carries a useful idea of a clear route back to activity, and leaves room to serve running, skiing, football, cycling, hiking, and recurring musculoskeletal problems. **Trittweise** is the warmer alternative. **Bewegungsgrund** is the more thoughtful and clinical alternative, but it needs a stronger legal and semantic check because it is built from a common word. None of these names is cleared. Reserve domains only after a professional trademark and company-name screen.

## What the name has to do

The name should:

- feel Swiss and considered without using Swiss clichés, mountains, peaks, or red-and-white visual shorthand;
- sound at home in Swiss Standard German and remain easy to say on a phone call;
- signal movement and progress without promising recovery, pain relief, or a guaranteed outcome;
- work for sport and active life now, then stretch to recurring back, neck, shoulder, and workday complaints;
- sit comfortably beside a serious descriptor, for example `Online-Physiotherapie für Sport und Bewegung`;
- be distinctive enough to search for and remember, while the descriptor captures terms such as `Online-Physiotherapie Schweiz`;
- look credible in a clinician's email signature, referral note, invoice, and website header.

The name should not carry the whole SEO burden. A branded name creates memory. The subtitle, page titles, copy, and clinician-authored advice pages explain the service and capture search intent.

## Naming principles from the current market

The visible Swiss market contains many literal combinations of `Physio`, `Sport`, `Therapie`, `Health`, and `Bewegung`. Examples include [Physio Sports](https://physiosports.ch/), [Physio Station](https://www.physio-station.ch/), [Bewegungsrausch](https://www.bewegungsrausch.ch/), [The Swiss Physio](https://theswissphysio.com/), and [Kinezi Sport Physiotherapie](https://www.sport-physiotherapie-zurich.ch/). These names are understandable, but the pattern is crowded and often ties the brand to a location, a practitioner, or sport alone.

The open position is a name with a human idea behind it: clarity, a next step, and the ability to keep moving. The practice can then state the clinical category directly in the descriptor. That gives people and search engines both pieces of information without making the brand sound interchangeable.

The naming direction also fits the existing **Bewegungsprotokoll** design concept. The website is organised around a clear next step, a written recommendation, and a measured return to activity. A name that suggests orientation or progress supports that system. A name that suggests a vague lifestyle promise would fight it.

## Shortlist

### 1. Klarlauf

`Klar` says what the service gives the person: orientation, a sensible next action, and less uncertainty. `Lauf` gives the name energy and an immediate link to sport without limiting the practice to runners. It is compact, works in a wordmark, and sounds natural when spoken: “Sie finden uns unter Klarlauf.” It also supports strong service language:

- `Klarlauf · Online-Physiotherapie für Bewegung in der Schweiz`
- `Klarlauf · Ihr nächster Schritt zurück in die Bewegung`
- `Online-Ersttermin bei Klarlauf`

Risk: `Lauf` may pull the first association toward running. The descriptor and the first two pages should visibly include skiing, cycling, football, hiking, and recurring pain so the brand does not narrow itself by accident.

### 2. Trittweise

`Trittweise` has warmth and restraint. It makes gradual progress feel like a sensible method, rather than a motivational slogan. It is easy to pronounce and has a good fit with the practice's measured care path. It also sounds native in Swiss Standard German. The weakness is ownability: it is an ordinary adverb and may be used by organisations in education, rehabilitation, or coaching even when no obvious physiotherapy result appears.

Possible lock-up: `Trittweise · Online-Physiotherapie für Sport und Alltag`.

### 3. Bewegungsgrund

This is the most conceptually rich option. In German, `Bewegungsgrund` can be heard as both a reason to move and the reason behind a movement problem. That double meaning suits a practice that listens before prescribing exercises. It is broader than sport and more credible than a performance-only name.

Risk: the compound is longer, and the meaning may need one supporting line. Search and trademark checks must cover the exact word, `Bewegungs Grund`, and close combinations. The practice should avoid suggesting that it diagnoses the cause from video alone.

Possible lock-up: `Bewegungsgrund · Physiotherapie für ein aktives Leben`.

### 4. Eigenraum

`Eigenraum` suggests room to move, personal agency, and care adapted to the person's actual home and activity. It could support sports, workday pain, and post-discharge continuity. It is the most ownable-feeling of the set, but also the least immediately clinical. The descriptor must appear close to the mark wherever the practice is introduced.

Possible lock-up: `Eigenraum · Online-Physiotherapie in der Schweiz`.

### 5. Tragpunkt

`Tragpunkt` carries a precise, grounded idea: the point from which a person can safely take the next load. It speaks to rehabilitation and strength without sounding like a gym or an app. It has a serious, almost architectural tone. That can work for premium Swiss care, but it is less warm and needs a human verbal identity around it.

Possible lock-up: `Tragpunkt · Physiotherapie für Bewegung und Belastbarkeit`.

## Recommendation

Take **Klarlauf** into the next clearance round. Keep **Trittweise** and **Bewegungsgrund** as live backups until a Swiss trademark search, company-name check, domain check, and native-speaker review are complete. If `Klarlauf` is blocked, choose `Trittweise` when warmth is the priority or `Bewegungsgrund` when clinical depth is the priority.

Do not launch the temporary `physio / vorschau` label as a customer-facing identity. Until the name is selected, use a neutral internal build label in metadata and preview tooling, while keeping the public page copy focused on the service.

## Preliminary conflict scan

This scan used exact-name web searches and current public Swiss registry entry points on 12 September 2026. It is a screening signal only. Search engines do not cover all registered marks, unindexed businesses, pending applications, or confusingly similar signs.

### Obvious names to remove from the shortlist

- **Bewegungswerk**: active Swiss physiotherapy businesses include [BewegungsWerk Bern GmbH](https://bewegungswerkbern.ch/) and [Bewegungswerk Da Silva](https://www.bewegungswerk-da-silva.ch/). Moneyhouse records the Bern company as active and founded in 2024. This is too close to the category and already occupied.
- **Bewegungsrausch**: an active Zurich physiotherapy and coaching practice uses [Bewegungsrausch](https://www.bewegungsrausch.ch/). It is memorable, but the name is already strongly associated with Swiss physiotherapy.
- **Körperklar**: [Körperklar-Life](https://www.koerperklar.de/) is an active fitness community and the word is also used by wellness businesses. It is too close to health and fitness uses.
- **Eigenkraft**: [Physiotherapie EigenKraft](https://www.physio-eigenkraft.de/) is an active physiotherapy practice. Do not use it as the lead candidate.
- **Nahdran**: [nahdran. Therapie](https://nahdran-therapie.de/) is an active German therapy practice. It is not Swiss, but the exact brand collision is clear.
- **WeiterRaum**: [WeiterRaum](https://www.wohnprojekte-portal.de/weiterraum/) is an existing German organisation and domain. The word also has strong non-medical uses.

### Searches that did not surface an obvious exact practice

Exact web queries for `Klarlauf`, `Trittweise` plus physiotherapy, `Bewegungsgrund` plus physiotherapy, `Eigenraum` plus physiotherapy, `Tragpunkt` plus physiotherapy, `Laufklar` plus physiotherapy, and `Schrittweite` plus physiotherapy did not return an obvious exact Swiss physiotherapy brand in the first results reviewed. This is a low-confidence negative signal, not proof of availability.

## Required clearance sequence

Complete these checks in order before committing design, domains, signage, or paid acquisition:

1. Search [Swissreg](https://www.swissreg.ch/database-client/home?lang=de) for each candidate, exact and similar, in the relevant Nice classes. Include likely health, medical, rehabilitation, education, software, and advertising classes with counsel.
2. Search [Zefix](https://www.zefix.ch/) and the cantonal commercial registers for exact and confusingly similar business names. A company-name entry does not equal trademark clearance.
3. Check the exact `.ch` name and sensible variants through [SWITCH Domain Name Lookup](https://www.nic.ch/de/whois/) or the [RDAP endpoint](https://www.nic.ch/de/whois/rdap/). A domain being free does not create trademark rights.
4. Search Google, Maps, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, and the Physioswiss directory for unregistered but established uses in healthcare and sports.
5. Ask a Swiss trademark attorney to run a similarity search and advise on filing. The Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property explains that Swiss trademark owners can prohibit identical or similar signs for the same or similar goods and services; see its [collision guidance](https://www.ige.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/schuetzen/marken/d/MB_Kennzeichen_D_2024.pdf).
6. Have at least two native Swiss Standard German speakers say each finalist aloud, write it from hearing, and explain what they expect the service to be. Test phone spelling and the plural or possessive forms.
7. Reserve the chosen `.ch`, `.com`, and key social handles only after the legal screen. Buy the domain through an accredited registrar listed by [SWITCH](https://www.nic.ch/registrars/).
