# Venture Vision

This project evaluates an online physiotherapy service for self paying and privately insured patients in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. The proposed experience is package based, remote, and digitally delivered.

The venture is not yet approved for launch. Market demand, country specific legal requirements, clinical suitability, practitioner obligations, privacy controls, acquisition economics, and competitive differentiation remain research questions. No claim in this document replaces advice from qualified legal or clinical professionals.

The first decision is whether a narrow, compliant launch wedge can produce evidence of willingness to pay and safe patient outcomes.
