## Editorial decision

The copy now speaks directly to the person seeking help. It uses short sentences, concrete actions, and Swiss spelling with `ss`. It avoids wellness slogans, inflated promises, false familiarity, translated English phrasing, and claims that the current product evidence does not support. The required CTA remains exactly `Online-Ersttermin buchen`.

## Language rules applied

- Swiss spelling throughout: `standardmässig`, `nächsten`, and no `ß`.
- Swiss Standard German only. No language selector, English interface copy, or translated launch language is implied in public copy.
- `Fachperson` is used consistently where a gender-neutral term is natural. Full clinician titles remain placeholders until verified.
- `Online-Ersttermin` is the product name. The copy uses «Betreuung per Video» where a repeated `Online-` compound would feel mechanical.
- English product nouns such as `Follow-ups`, `Online-Assessment`, and `Return to Activity` are not used in the public copy.
- Public launch routes use Swiss German slugs: `/sport/laufen`, `/sport/skifahren`, `/sport/fussball`, `/sport/wandern`, and `/termin-buchen`.
- No «Du», playful language, exclamation marks, motivational slogans, invented testimonials, outcome guarantees, or unverified numbers.
- The copy does not describe stock subjects as patients or clinicians.

## Copy that remains intentionally gated

The following placeholders must be replaced by named, approved facts before publication:

- clinician name, title, training, Swiss authorisation or registration, languages, and biography;
- confirmed canton service region and the rule for the patient's location at each appointment;
- price, payment method, validity, cancellation and refund terms, invoice or receipt details;
- clinical safety wording, red flags, emergency instruction, and referral route;
- privacy notice, processors, storage and retention details, and video recording wording;
- private-treatment answer about diagnosis or prescription requirements;
- verified contact address and response window.

These are evidence gates, not copy gaps to fill with plausible wording. No claim of suitability, reimbursement, safety, result, or professional status should be strengthened until its owner has approved the exact wording.
