# Website brief: Swiss online physiotherapy

**Status:** Design and content draft, publication blocked pending product, clinical, legal, and operational sign-off
**Date:** 12 September 2026
**Market:** Switzerland only, German-speaking Switzerland first
**Launch language:** Deutsch, Schweizer Standardsprache (de-CH), with Swiss spelling. No French, Italian, English, language selector, or translated launch pages.
**Audience:** Self-paying and privately insured active adults
**Primary conversion:** Paid online assessment
**Source documents:** [Swiss website PRD](../prd/01_swiss_online_physio_website.md), [Swiss market memo](../research/switzerland/2026-09-swiss-online-physiotherapy-market.md), [reference direction](REFERENCE_DIRECTION.md), [venture index](../venture/README.md)

## Brief in one paragraph

Create a calm, editorial Swiss health practice website for active people who want to understand what they can safely do next and return to the activity that matters to them. The first offer is a clinician-led 60-minute Online-Ersttermin, followed where suitable by a six-week package called Zurück in die Bewegung. The site must make the human practitioner, Swiss availability, price, suitability limits, safety route, privacy basics, and booking step clear before asking for sensitive information. The voice is warm, serious, direct, and caring. The design feels measured and premium through typography, space, photography, and precise interaction, never through novelty or decoration.

## Evidence and claim rules

## Positioning

**Positioning statement:** Online physiotherapy for active people in Switzerland who want a clear recovery plan, a qualified professional, and accountability through the next step.

**Distinctive promise:** Make the next safe movement decision clear, then help the person follow through.

**Reasons to believe:** Named Swiss physiotherapists; sport and movement competence; one clinician across the journey where possible; a written next step; explicit online limits and referral route; transparent CHF pricing; privacy explained in plain language.

**What the website must not imply:** universal suitability, guaranteed recovery, pain-free results, compulsory-insurance reimbursement, diagnosis from video alone, emergency care, hands-on treatment, or that stock photography depicts a patient or clinician.

## Audience and jobs

The first audience is German-speaking Swiss adults who run, cycle, ski, play football, hike, climb, or train recreationally. Their trigger is pain, a non-operative injury, a return after a break, or uncertainty about training load. They need to decide what to do this week and how to progress. They want confidence without false certainty, continuity without repeated explanations, and care that fits work and home life.

The first adjacent test is desk-based professionals with recurring back, neck, or shoulder pain. It gets a dedicated page only after the sports offer has evidence. Later audiences, such as post-discharge continuity, English-speaking professionals, or pelvic health, need separate clinical capability and operating checks. Any future language expansion is deferred until the German launch has evidence.

## Offer and conversion model

### Primary offer: Online-Ersttermin

**Internal price hypothesis:** CHF 169, pending checkout test. Do not display publicly until approved.
**Format:** 60-minute secure video appointment with one named physiotherapist.
**Includes:** structured history and goals, suitability and red-flag screen, movement observation appropriate to video, plain-language explanation of findings and limits, and a written next step within the confirmed response window.

**CTA:** `Online-Ersttermin buchen`

### Continuation offer: Zurück in die Bewegung

**Internal price hypothesis:** CHF 549, pending checkout test. Do not display publicly until approved.
**Format:** six weeks.
**Includes:** one Online-Ersttermin, four 30-minute Folgetermine, one named activity goal, personalised plan, weekly adjustment, asynchronous video feedback within a stated window, progress review, and next-step recommendation.

**Exclusions shown at purchase:** emergency access, unlimited messaging, hands-on examination or treatment, imaging, and guaranteed outcomes.

### Conversion journey

1. Visitor lands on a sport or movement problem and recognises the situation.
2. Home or page explains the service and shows the paid first step, price, duration, and online limit.
3. Visitor opens a short suitability flow with consent before health questions.
4. Suitable visitor books and pays. Unsuitable or urgent visitor receives approved escalation guidance before payment.
5. The Online-Ersttermin creates a written next step. The clinician offers the package only when clinically suitable.

Do not force a sales call. A 15-minute fit conversation can be offered as a secondary route only if the team can staff it and measure whether it helps.

### FAQ `/fragen`

Use native-reviewed answers, 40–90 words each.

- **Ist Online-Physiotherapie für mich geeignet?** Das klären Sie im Online-Ersttermin. Eine Betreuung per Video kann passen, wenn Ihre Situation sicher beurteilt und begleitet werden kann. Wenn Sie eine Untersuchung oder Behandlung vor Ort brauchen, sagen wir Ihnen das und empfehlen den nächsten Schritt.
- **Brauche ich eine Diagnose oder Verordnung?** Für den privaten Online-Ersttermin [bestätigte Antwort einsetzen]. Eine Diagnose ersetzt die Eignungsprüfung nicht.
- **Was passiert beim ersten Termin?** Sie besprechen, was passiert ist, was Sie wieder tun möchten und was Sie bisher versucht haben. Die Fachperson beobachtet geeignete Bewegungen, erklärt, was per Video möglich ist, und empfiehlt den nächsten Schritt.

- **Übernimmt meine Zusatzversicherung die Kosten?** Das hängt von Ihrer Police und einer schriftlichen Bestätigung durch die Versicherung ab. Bitte klären Sie dies vor dem Kauf.
- **Übernimmt die Grundversicherung die Kosten?** Eine Kostenübernahme ist für dieses private Angebot nicht zugesichert. Bitte verlassen Sie sich nicht auf eine Erstattung, bevor sie schriftlich bestätigt ist.

## Visual system

**Register:** Swiss precision with human warmth. The page is a brand surface, but its distinctive character comes from how it explains a real consultation, not from decoration.

**Design idea: the consultation sheet.** Treat each major section as a page from a well-made clinical working document: one observation, one decision, one next action. A thin datum rule aligns the sections and carries a small functional marker such as `01 / Ersttermin`, `02 / Einschätzung`, or `03 / Nächster Schritt`. These markers are navigation aids, never ornamental labels. The rule stops at the content measure and never becomes a background pattern. This gives the site a recognisable structure that a generic clinic template cannot inherit without losing its logic.

**First-screen composition:** At 1280px and above, use a 5/7 split. The left column holds the eyebrow, H1, short body, one CTA, and a compact line with duration, confirmed Swiss region, and approved price. The right column holds one portrait-oriented movement photograph in a quiet crop, plus a small caption that identifies it as illustrative while stock remains. Under the text, place a single proof sentence with the named responsible professional and checked title. Do not add a logo cloud, statistic row, floating badge, or second CTA. At 360–768px, the order is: identity and region, H1, image, body, price line, CTA, proof sentence. The CTA must appear before the first scroll break.

**Page composition:** Home has seven deliberate beats: arrival and offer, the four-step care path, one “what we can and cannot assess online” spread, the named practitioner, the package details, safety/privacy, and questions. Use full-width colour changes only twice, at the practitioner and safety sections. Build the process as a vertical reading path with one large numeral and one sentence per step, not four equal cards. The package is a bordered document-like panel with a visible total and inclusions, followed by exclusions in plain text. Finish with one quiet booking band. Every section has one visual anchor and one action at most.

**Palette:** warm paper `oklch(97% 0.012 90)`; deep blue-green ink `oklch(24% 0.035 205)`; blue-grey quiet text that passes body contrast; one restrained alpine green or mineral blue accent; amber or red only for safety and errors. Tune in browser and test WCAG 2.2 AA. Avoid pure white or black, gradients, neon sport colours, purple-blue, glass effects, and generic medical blue.

**Type:** Use one editorial display face with a visible Swiss or humanist character, paired with a restrained grotesk for interface text. Newsreader plus Inter or Source Sans 3 is an acceptable test pairing, not an automatic decision. Compare at least three licensed pairings using the actual de-CH headlines before locking. Display 56/1.02 desktop and 40/1.08 mobile; section 34/1.1 and 28/1.15; body 18/1.55 desktop and 17/1.55 mobile; labels 13/1.3. Give German copy 30% expansion room. Use sentence case, optical sizing, modest tracking, and no all-caps paragraphs. The display face belongs to the patient-facing argument; the grotesk belongs to controls, prices, dates, and clinical metadata.

**Grid and rhythm:** Use a 12-column desktop grid with a 58–68 character reading measure. Anchor the left edge of every heading, image caption, and action to the same two columns. Let photographs cross one grid boundary only when the caption remains attached to them. Use an 8px base rhythm with section spacing at 64, 96, or 128px, chosen by content length. On mobile, use one column with 20px side padding, 32px between related groups, and 72px between major sections. Do not centre-align paragraphs or create symmetrical empty boxes to fill a grid.

**Shape and depth:** Use 1px hairlines and no card shadow on the core page. Radius is 0px for structural document panels, 8px for controls, and 16px for photographs only. This controlled difference separates information from touch targets and keeps the page from becoming a field of rounded tiles. If a shadow is needed for a menu or modal, use one soft blue-green shadow from the same light source. Never use a floating dashboard surface.

**Motion:** The datum marker may draw in once as the section enters view, 240ms ease-out. Image crops may settle by 160ms on load. Buttons change colour and border without moving adjacent content. Booking progress uses an explicit step indicator and preserves entered values. Nothing essential waits for animation. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`, keyboard focus, and touch input.

## Reference analysis and anti-slop guardrails

Borrow the argument order from Sword Health, the separate proof layer from Kaia Health, the visible human and geographic origin from Neko Health, and the cash-pay transparency and provider introduction from Modern Roots Healthcare. Borrow no visual template. The site’s own point of view is the consultation sheet: the visitor always sees where they are, what is being assessed, and what decision comes next. Sources are listed in [REFERENCE_DIRECTION.md](REFERENCE_DIRECTION.md).

Reject the exact generic patterns found in the first draft: a hero image paired with an interchangeable serif headline; a trust strip that could belong to any clinic; four identical process cards; a three-column feature row; rounded package tiles with equal visual weight; oversized abstract movement curves; and a footer that repeats every CTA. Replace each with the grid datum, attached captions, one dominant offer panel, and a process path tied to the actual care journey. No smiling stock clinician pointing at a laptop, fake metrics, anonymous reviews, insurer logos, certification badges without permission, invented quotes, outcome guarantees, floating blobs, purple gradients, glass cards, or stock subjects framed as patients or clinicians.

## UI components and states

- Header: logo at the datum line, five compact links, and one primary CTA. The header becomes a two-row compact bar only after scroll, with the CTA retaining its width and label. Mobile uses a full-height sheet with a clear close action, focus trap, escape key, and the same reading order as desktop.
- Hero: 5/7 composition described above. The image has one solid caption panel outside the crop. The price line is adjacent to the CTA, never hidden behind a tooltip. No text directly over uncontrolled photography.
- Proof sentence: one named person, checked title, and confirmed Swiss service region. Keep it as text with a link to the practitioner page until a real seal or partner mark has been verified.
- Care path: four numbered stops on a single vertical rule. Each stop has a state, `offen`, `im Gespräch`, or `nächster Schritt`, only when the product actually exposes that state. On mobile the rule becomes a left edge with 24px clearance, never a decorative timeline.
- Offer panel: a single dominant panel with total price, duration, appointment count, inclusions, exclusions, suitability note, and CTA. Use a two-column interior on desktop and a stacked document on mobile. Do not place assessment and package in equal cards; the assessment is the safe first action.
- Practitioner profile: a real portrait only when approved, cropped at eye level with visible room context. Put proof metadata beside the portrait and a short method paragraph below. No circular avatar, floating credential badge, or carousel.
- Safety and privacy: a full-width ink or paper section with a clear heading, icon, and text. Safety uses an icon, heading, and text in addition to colour. Emergency guidance is visually separated from routine suitability guidance.
- FAQ: native details elements or equivalent keyboard-accessible disclosure, deep-linkable by question, with multiple items allowed open. The open state must not shift the primary CTA into or under the viewport edge.
- Suitability flow: one question per step for sensitive data, consent first, clear back and exit, a visible step count, and no raw answers sent to analytics. The unsuitable and urgent states are full-width decision pages with the next action above the fold.
- Booking edge states: `Online-Betreuung passt für Ihre Angaben derzeit nicht. Bitte folgen Sie der Empfehlung und wenden Sie sich an [geprüfter Kontakt]. Es wurde kein Termin gebucht.` For urgent cases show `[freigegebene Notfallanweisung]`. For no availability show `Zurzeit sind keine passenden Termine verfügbar. Wählen Sie einen neuen Termin oder wenden Sie sich an [geprüfter Kontakt].` For cancellation show `Ihr Termin wurde storniert. **Content accessibility:** Every clinician or explanatory video has captions and a transcript. Every image has descriptive alt text that states what is visible without implying treatment, diagnosis, or outcome; decorative images use empty alt. Validation errors are specific, placed beside the field, and announced to assistive technology. Warnings use icon, heading, and text in addition to colour. Test `Online-Ersttermin buchen` at 360px and 200% zoom.
- Booking: availability, patient location, payment, consent, cancellation, confirmation, reminders, retry and no-availability states.
- Form states: rest, hover, focus, active, disabled, validation error, loading, payment failure, success, reschedule, and cancellation.
