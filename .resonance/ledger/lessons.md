## les-clinical-content-needs-evidence: Sports condition pages need bounded expert evidence
type: lesson
created: 2026-09-12
status: active
confidence: high
review_due: 2026-12-12

Research supports selected sports and musculoskeletal topics, but it does not prove Steepstep outcomes or make every condition suitable for video care. Each condition page must identify what the problem can mean, what assessment and loading work may involve, a realistic range of progression, warning signs and the point at which local in-person care is needed. A named practitioner must review clinical copy before it is treated as final.

## les-image-trust-requires-licensing-and-variety: Sport imagery must be distinct and cleared
type: lesson
created: 2026-09-12
status: active
confidence: high
review_due: 2026-12-12

Images carry clinical trust only when they visibly fit physiotherapy, are not repeated without a reason, and have a recorded commercial-use basis with photographer and model rights where relevant. Powerflow imagery may be reused only within the permission and rights record. Stock imagery must never imply a Steepstep practitioner, patient result or clinical outcome.

## les-remote-care-boundary: Video care is selective, not a substitute for every examination
type: lesson
created: 2026-09-12
status: active
confidence: high
review_due: 2026-12-12

Online physiotherapy can support history-taking, movement observation, exercise instruction, load planning and follow-up for suitable presentations. It cannot provide hands-on examination, imaging, emergency assessment or every post-trauma decision. The site must make that boundary visible and route unsuitable or urgent cases to local medical care.

## les-public-release-gates: Finished copy still needs clinical and operational proof
type: lesson
created: 2026-09-12
status: active
confidence: high
review_due: 2026-12-12

Green build and security checks do not establish live mail delivery, practitioner credential evidence, canton-specific operating details or the commercial result of pricing. Keep those proof obligations visible in the release ledger, while the public site itself contains no preview or unfinished wording.
